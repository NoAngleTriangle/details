Welcome to Details!

Details is a small group that does many things through our subgroups. Currently, our only major public subgroup is Delroz, our game development branch. However, we have many other subgroups still in development that will be public soon...
We also have other projects, and a list of these projects may be found here.


How was Details founded? What is our history?

Details was founded by NoAngleTriangle and Jayce Z. Glenn on (or around) September 26, 2022 at a local elementary school in Florida, United States.
We were founded in the days before Hurricane Ian made landfall on Florida.
Jayce Glenn and NoAngleTriangle were friends at that time and were in fourth grade. After lunch at school, Jayce showed NoAngleTriangle a list of facts he created about Hurricane Ian on a sheet of paper.
Jayce and NoAngleTriangle then created more papers of these facts about Hurricane Ian. They wanted to sell these papers, but unfortunately never did.
NoAngleTriangle called this operation "Jayce and [NoAngleTriangle's name]". A few days after Hurricane Ian when school was back, Jayce named it "Details".
We were named "Details" because of the way we structured our papers about Hurricane Ian. They were structured something like:
"Detail 1: Hurricane Ian will hit us in a few days
 Detail 2: We won't be at school during Hurricane Ian."
